# Naija News Digest — User guide

A simple guide for readers of the digest.

## 1. What it is

Naija News Digest is a **daily, ad-free roundup of the top Nigerian news stories**:

- the **top stories** of the day, grouped into **Politics, National, Sports, Business, Technology** and **Other**;
- each story as a short **2–3 sentence summary** plus a one-line **"why it matters"**;
- a **link to the original article** on the source site;
- **exchange rates** (USD/GBP/EUR → NGN) at the top;
- an **archive** of every past daily digest.

It is for information only — not investment or legal advice.

## 2. Opening the site

Go to **https://naija-news.pages.dev** (works on phone or computer). No sign-in is required.

## 3. The home page

### Header
"**Naija News Digest — YYYY-MM-DD — Top stories by category**" tells you which day the digest covers.

### Exchange rates
A box at the top shows the current **USD/NGN, GBP/NGN and EUR/NGN** rates and when they were last updated. These are reference rates from the open exchange-rate API.

### Sections
Stories are grouped into sections: **Politics, National, Sports, Business, Technology, Other**. Each story card shows:
- the **headline** and its **category** badge;
- the **source(s)** that covered it (a story covered by several outlets is usually more important);
- the **publication date**;
- a **2–3 sentence summary**;
- **"Why it matters"** — a one-line explanation;
- a **"Read original →"** link to the source article.

### Subscribe
If a **"Subscribe to the daily email"** button appears, click it to join the Beehiiv newsletter and get the digest in your inbox.

### Archive
Click **"Past roundups →"** (top right) to browse every previous daily digest.

## 4. The archive page

- Lists all past digests, newest first, as `YYYY-MM-DD` links.
- Click a date to open that day's full digest (same layout as the home page).
- Use your browser's back button to return to the latest.

## 5. Reading tips

- **Stories are deduplicated** — if several outlets cover the same event, they appear once, with all sources listed.
- **The same story won't repeat** across days — once a story has been featured, it's excluded from future digests.
- **"Why it matters"** is an AI-generated take; treat it as a starting point, not a verdict. Always follow the **"Read original"** link for the full story.
- Exchange rates are indicative and may differ slightly from what your bank quotes.

## 6. Getting the digest by RSS

Subscribe in any feed reader to **https://naija-news.pages.dev/feed.xml**. Each daily roundup appears as a new item (one per day), linking to that day's full digest.

## 7. Getting the digest by email

Subscribe via the **"Subscribe to the daily email"** button on the home page. You'll receive one email per day (around 01:00 UTC). To unsubscribe, use the link at the bottom of any email.

## 8. Feedback and corrections

Found an error, a missing story, or a miscategorised item? The digest is generated automatically, so the best fix is to adjust the sources or ranking — see the [Owner's guide](owner-guide.md) or [Developer guide](developer-guide.md).
